Deepali Madan

CEO & Head of Sales

Deepali Madan

Deepali is the CEO & Head- Sales of Olive Ridley Advisory (ORA) Group. Her vision is to have ORA drive the culture of decision-making based on the comprehensive & advanced Analytics solutions to Ensure Roadmap to Success and empower the clients to make a difference by realigning the possibilities at a faster pace and providing an edge over competitors. Deepali has over 15+ years of experience in strategy consulting & conferences in the corporate world in US, Europe, China and India across diverse sectors. She is an Advisors to start ups; Ideated, executed and analyzed various market strategies to scale business & led the company to profitability within two years of operations. She led the global teams and successfully delivered market access, GTM, competitive intelligence, process play books, market sizing, LCM strategy, asset value proposition, BD&L strategies, patient advocacy, Label & situational analysis, knowledge books, new product launch, real time dashboards, augmented successful delivery of customized LMS system, agile deliveries. She managed the pre-clinical to early stage asset development program in oncology, pain & inflammation and Alzheimer's disease areas. She successfully delivered in-silico and in-vitro discovery of two oncology assets and managed first stage development of smart polymeric based drug delivery system. She holds engineering in Biotechnology, DPU with MBA- Pharmaceutical Management from ICFAI Business School. She was an advisor to National Disaster Management Authority, India during the H1N1 pandemic outbreak, and has 2 publications.

John Ellis

Strategy advisor & Head of Portfolio growth

John Ellis

John is a leader in our Aerospace practice and is also a member of ORA’s Global Leadership Team. He is an expert in growth strategy and has helped many companies within the aviation sector across the globe including UK, USA, Canada, Oman, Qatar, India, and UAE and beyond to define and execute on ways to grow and create value. He has led several strategic and transformational engagements for clients, including growth strategy, value proposition development and buy- and sell-side transaction support. He specializes in developing regional strategy, competitive strategy, M&A strategy, and new plant investment. A leader of ORA’s work on ESG and sustainability strategy development, he advises clients on taking a holistic, commercial approach to sustainability and making the right strategic choices to realize value creation. He is the founder of London-based, Ellis Capital Partners. Ellis Capital Partners is currently helping to fund projects in the UK, Canada, Hong Kong, India, Malaysia, Sri Lanka, Thailand, and Vietnam. He is the author of “The 28 Golden Rules of Personal and Business Success, NLP Techniques for Incredible Success, Understanding UK Export Finance, and Problem Solving with GROW Techniques. John has appeared on Sky News and BBC News to talk about aviation issues. He served in various positions across career trajectory includes CEO - Middle East Aerospace, CMO - Samad Aerospace, Commercial Manager – Fujairah International Airport, Director - UK Government for Aerospace, Dubai, Director – Farnborough Aerospace. John holds a masters from Aviation Economics and Finance- London School of Economics & Finance

Dr. Srinivasa K. Rao

Head of Portfolio Strategy & Development (Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals)

Dr. Srinivasa K. Rao

Dr. Srinivasa K. Rao is an Advisor & Head of Portfolio strategy & development, North America and Europe region at ORA Group. He focuses on the biopharmaceutical, medical device and life sciences sector, and has experience across multiple therapeutic areas and industry segments. Srinivasa has been at the forefront of R&D innovation, Commercial excellence, New Drug/ Vaccine launch for over 30 years. Srinivasa is the founder of Granova Naturals & Indian Institute of Biotechnology, a think tank committed to helping organizations in the public and private sector accelerate their rate of innovation & promote indigenous medicinal knowledge and deliver the best candidates for making better and affordable drugs. He published over 30 peer-reviewed scientific articles, presented, participated, organized, chaired, and co-chaired several scientific meetings over the last 25 years in France, USA, and India and holds 3 (related) U.S. patents and a few are in the pipeline.
