Origin

ORA Group has emerged from Sciencstein Edutech who began consulting operations in the Education, Trade, Technology, & Operations in 2015. Our management has two decades of consulting experience & we are passionate about helping organizations improve their knowledge to provide a competitive advantage for the business & ensure growth to meet their revenue goals. We have never been satisfied with the status quo & always been zealous about developing new methodologies, strategies and processes that enable us to provide best-in-class research and innovative analytical tools from a wide range of sources. ORA’s business relationships & industry legacy with regulatory bodies & regulators strengthen our client credence in us. Our team comprises a team of seasoned professionals with an average experience of 7-15 years, 50+ experts & 20+ veterans with 25 years of industry experience.

Who we are

Olive Ridley Advisory (ORA) Group is a leading global management consulting, Market Intelligence & analytics, business conferences, & advisory group. ORA partners with decision-makers to address their unique & paramount challenges with an innovative framework and provide actionable insights to make informed decisions. We're here to champion the maneuverability definition & shape the possible.

Underpinned by a sustainable & transformational business framework is a blend of connoisseur's expertise and powered by Advanced Analytics, Artificial intelligence/ML to transform challenges & crises into opportunities. We work closely with clients to consistently make better decisions to seize competitive advantage across the whole spectrum regionally & globally to achieve growth by enhancing their resilience, assessing well-timed risks, customer engagement, leading-edge innovative solutions, mergers & acquisitions, pricing strategy, lean implementation, maximizing market share, regulatory collaborations for compliance & protecting their IP rights, etc.
