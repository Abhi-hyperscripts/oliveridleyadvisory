Privacy Statement

Introduction

Olive Ridley Advisory and its affiliates (defined as “Olive Ridley Advisory”, “ORA”, “US”, “WE” or “OUR”, as appropriate) values and appreciate the trust you place in us to protect and store your distinctive data carefully and in accordance with applicable laws. This privacy policy sets out details of Olive Ridley Advisory’s information gathering and dissemination practices. In this privacy policy, references to “YOU”/ ”YOUR” shall be references to any person submitting any data to Olive Ridley Advisory., Whether via our website – www.oliveridleyadvisory.com(“website”) or otherwise.


Information storage

Company may collect personal information from customers, clients, employees, contractors and other individuals when it is necessary for business purposes. Usually, we collect information like the contact details and organizational roles of our clients, vendors, TPC and other business contacts. Typically, this information includes names, addresses, contact numbers, email addresses and job titles. In the course of providing professional services to our clients, we may need to collect more detailed distinctive information (for instance, financial details if we are engaged to perform financial or tax services).

We collect, retain in our database, and use the information voluntarily provided by you. Olive Ridley Advisory receives limited personal information, such as name, title, company address, email address, contact number and fax numbers from website visitors, customers, members or related. Demographic information, including occupation & gender, is not actively sought, but may be submitted when a customer responds to an online job application form or registers for our Corporate Member or Pedagogue Program. It is our policy to limit the information collected to solely the minimum information requisite to complete a visitor's request. Customers are also able to send emails through this site. Distinctive Information submitted to us is only available to employees managing this information for purposes of contacting you or sending you emails based on your request for information and to contracted service providers/third-party/partners for purposes of providing services relating to our communications with you. Because we tend to use the website as a recruiting tool, a visit to the website may also result in the visitor sending a resume to an individual within ORA which may contain additional detailed information.


** We will never sell or rent your distinctive information to third parties for their use without your consent. Company may share your contact & other information with our partners for limited purposes, with certain third parties (who may only use the information for the purpose for which it was provided), and when explicitly instructed by the court of law.


Information usage

The key purposes of collecting personal information about individuals are as follows:

  • To facilitate your use and personalizing your experience on the website
  • To Fulfill orders, ship and confirm orders and manage payment collection & related service at your disposal.
  • To process and respond to your inquiries and other communications
  • Maintaining contact with clients
  • Keeping clients and other contacts informed of the services we offer and industry developments that may be of interest to them, and to notify them of service offerings, webinars, seminars and other events we organize
  • General management and reporting purposes, such as invoicing and account management
  • Recruitment of staff and providing internal services to them
  • All other lawful purposes related to our business and in furtherance to fulfilment of lawful contract.
  • Distinctive information collected by company through this process is used only for the intended reason stated at the time that the information is collected or for the purposes specified above.

 

Disclosure of personal information

 Company will never sell or rent your personal information to third parties for their use without your consent & may disclose your contact, other information with our certified partners for limited purposes, with certain third parties (who may only use the information for the purpose for which it was provided), and when explicitly instructed by the court of law.

  • such disclosure or transfer is permitted under this privacy statement or the disclosure or transfer required by law, including for the purpose of compliance with subpoenas, court orders, requests or order from legal authorities or law enforcement agencies requiring such disclosure;
  • such disclosure is required for us to use or process the data for the purpose for which it was obtained.

·        Except as set out in this privacy statement or specifically allowed under law, company will not disclose, share, sell or rent any customer personal information to any third party. If an individual wishes to update or change any information, customers may do so by requesting & company will prospectively cease to use or modify his or her personal information.


Retention of your Personal information

We will keep your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or either required to by law.

 

Unsubscribing From Email Communications

  • We only collect and hold your email address in order to send you the information and services you have requested. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Company has designed a mechanism of subscription via email mentioned at the end of the email body, in case of any issues kindly contact our customer care email mentioned on the webpage.


European Privacy Rights

If you are in the European Union, you can make a data access or data deletion request by emailing [email protected] with “GDPR Request” in the subject line. Include your email address and the nature of your request. We will respond to your request within 48 hours.


Transfer of Personal Information Overseas

  • We may share your personal information to companies within the OLIVE RIDLEY ADVISORY group to carry out services and to send you marketing communications including for surveys and marketing activities. Some of these group companies are based outside the EEA.
  • As a result, we may transfer personal information overseas to other countries whose privacy laws provide different level of protection.
  • We will ensure that we have measures in place with these third parties to ensure that your personal information is kept secure.

 

Cookies and Log files

Cookies may be used on some pages of our websites. When you visit our website, OLIVE RIDLEY ADVISORY’s servers (which may be hosted by a third-party service provider) may collect information indirectly and automatically about your activities on the website. Cookies are small text files placed on a visitor’s hard drive that assist us in providing a more customised website experience. We use cookies to ensure that we give you the best experience on our website and to show you content that is relevant to you. By continuing to surf on the website, you explicitly agree on the use of cookies on this website. You will have seen a show/pop up to this effect on your primary visit to this website, although it will not usually appear on subsequent or next visits you can control cookies by changing your browser settings. If you decline our cookie settings, you may not be able to access all our site features. By continuing to use our website-www.oliveridleyadvisory.com you are agreeing to our use of cookies. Cookies are primarily used to enhance the visitor’s online or virtual experience & are not used to track the navigational habits of identified visitors, unless we obtain the visitor’s specific and prior consent to do so.


Recruitment Activities

When you use our online Career platform, we collect the personal data that you willingly submit while applying for jobs, and we use it for recruitment-related activities.  We use your personal information to share relevant information about careers and opportunities with us and to analyze the effectiveness of our recruitment efforts and resources. More information about how we will use candidate information during the recruitment mechanism will be provided as part of the recruitment process.


Age

The Website is not directed toward children under 18 years of age nor does OLIVE RIDLEY ADVISORY knowingly collect information from children under 18 on the website or offline. If you are under 18, you are not permitted to register for an account on the website. If you provide information to OLIVE RIDLEY ADVISORY through the Site or offline, you represent that you are 18 years of age or older.


Security/Safeguards

OLIVE RIDLEY ADVISORY has implemented generally accepted standards of technology and operational security in order to protect personal information from loss, misuse, alteration or destruction. We regularly monitor our systems to mitigate possible vulnerabilities and attacks.
